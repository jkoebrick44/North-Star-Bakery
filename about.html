Who we are, and what we strive for.

We are a small business that loves to create bread with our hands and love to make memories with the new customers that enter our store. We strive on the brining happiness to everyones lives. We love to help the community anway we can.

Our Sourcing

We believe delicious baking starts with thoughtfully chosen ingredients. We aim to source fresh, high-quality ingredients from local farmers and trusted suppliers whenever possible. By choosing seasonal fruits, prioritizing responsibly produced ingredients, and buying thoughtfully to reduce waste, we strive to make treats we’re proud to share with our community.
